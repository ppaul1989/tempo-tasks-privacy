TEMPO / TASKS

Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Developer: Paweł Brzeziński
Privacy and support: apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com

Tempo Tasks is an Android task list. This policy describes how the app and its advertising service handle data.

Tasks and reminders

Task titles, priorities, due dates, completion status, and completion timing history are stored in a private database on your device. The Stats view calculates completion speed, daily carryover, and overdue counts there. Reminder scheduling and notifications are handled on the device. The developer does not receive task contents or task statistics. The app does not require an account or synchronize tasks to a server. Android backup is disabled for this app. Deleting an individual task removes its title and details; untitled timing history may remain for aggregate statistics. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

If you set an arrival reminder, address queries may be sent to Android's geocoding service as you type so the app can show matching addresses. You choose a resolved address before saving. The selected place and coordinates are stored in the app's private database. With your permission, Google Play services uses precise location to monitor the place and notify Tempo Tasks when you arrive. Background location permission is needed for arrival reminders while the app is closed. You can deny or revoke location access in Android settings; ordinary tasks and time reminders still work. The developer does not receive your location or place names, and Tempo Tasks does not pass them to the advertising SDK.

If you allow notification permission, the app may display due-task reminders and a morning briefing. You may also turn on the optional Gentle coach in Stats for occasional daytime task prompts; it starts off. You can turn it off in the app or change notification permission in Android settings. Task titles may appear in unlocked notifications, while public lock-screen previews hide them.

Advertising and consent

Tempo Tasks contains one banner ad placement below the task list. It is shown only when at least three tasks are open and Google’s consent SDK permits ad requests. No interstitial, app-open, or rewarded ads are used. The app does not pass task titles or due dates to the ad SDK.

Tempo Tasks uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage privacy choices. According to Google’s SDK disclosure, the Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP address, app interaction data, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The advertising ID may be included when available. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. Google and its advertising partners process data under their own policies; see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Where required, the app asks for advertising consent before requesting ads and provides an Ad choices control to revisit those choices. If consent status cannot be checked, the app keeps the task list available and does not request an ad during that launch. You can also manage advertising ID and app permissions in Android settings.

Sharing and retention

The developer does not sell task data or operate a task-data server. Task titles and details remain on your device until you delete the task or uninstall the app. Untitled timing history remains for aggregate statistics until you uninstall the app. Advertising data is sent to Google and its partners when ads are requested; Google controls its retention and deletion practices under its policies.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its advertising setup changes. The effective date above will be revised. For privacy questions, contact apps.pbrzezinski@gmail.com.
